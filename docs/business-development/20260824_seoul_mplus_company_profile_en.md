## Slide 2. Problem — rental candidate review is repetitive and fragmented

- Tenants repeatedly search listings and re-enter the same requirements.
- Operators manually compare budget, move-in timing, and lifestyle constraints.
- When decision reasons are not recorded, review and handover become difficult.
- Sharing personal data too early increases privacy and compliance risk.

**PoC question:** Can structured profiles and explainable ranking reduce review time and missing information?

## Slide 3. Product — show the score, the reason, and the constraint

1. A tenant enters budget, preferred locations, move-in date, and lifestyle needs.
2. Rentme compares the profile with listing requirements across budget, credit/trust inputs, timing, lifestyle, and employment conditions.
3. The result includes a score breakdown, human-readable reasons, and explicit `dealbreakers`.
4. An operator reviews the recommendation and can continue to a conversation.

Suggested visual: profile input → ranked match → reason and dealbreaker. Use only approved screenshots of working features.

## Slide 4. Differentiation — an operator workflow, not another listing search

| Conventional listing search | Rentme workflow |
|---|---|
| Starts with available properties | Starts with structured tenant needs |
| Focuses on filter pass/fail | Shows score, reasons, and unmet constraints |
| Relies on manual comparison | Provides a repeatable review framework |
| May assume early data integration | Starts with synthetic and de-identified data |

Rentme does not claim to provide credit assessment, automated contracting, or legal decisions. The operator remains the final decision-maker.

## Slide 5. Proposed four-week PoC with FPT

- **Week 1:** Map the current workflow and define the data boundary.
- **Week 2:** Localize 30 synthetic tenant profiles and 30 synthetic listings.
- **Week 3:** Run matching and collect operator evaluations.
- **Week 4:** Compare the baseline, document regulatory and product gaps, and make a joint follow-up decision.

Evaluation metrics: median review time, Top-N agreement with operator judgment, missing-field detection rate, and explanation clarity. Targets will be agreed only after the current baseline is confirmed.
